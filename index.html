Atelier Digital

Private market research and digital product development tool

Atelier Digital is a private tool used to evaluate digital product opportunities using authorized marketplace information, trend signals, and search-demand evidence.

DATA & API USAGE

Responsible data use.

  • Uses authorized APIs and public data sources.
  • Does not scrape marketplaces.
  • Does not estimate private competitor sales.
  • Does not resell marketplace data.
  • Access is limited to the owner of the application.

PURPOSE

Build with evidence.

Research, validation and development of digital products for online marketplaces.
