About Atelier Digital

Atelier Digital is a private market-research and digital-product development tool created for the personal use of its owner. It is not currently offered to the public as a service or software-as-a-service product.

Information used by the application

Atelier Digital may use authorized APIs and data sources, including the Google Ads API, to obtain permitted market information such as keyword information, available search-volume metrics, competition indicators, and other metrics made available by those services.

This information is used only to analyze market trends, research potential digital-product opportunities, support the owner’s internal decisions, and improve Atelier Digital’s analyses.

Use of Google API data

  • Atelier Digital does not sell data obtained through the Google Ads API.
  • Google Ads API data is not used to create advertising profiles about third parties.
  • The data is used for the owner’s internal research and analysis.
  • Use of information received from Google APIs is intended to comply with applicable Google policies and terms.

Storage and security

Market-research data may be retained locally or within the private infrastructure required to operate the application. Reasonable technical measures are used to protect that infrastructure. API keys, OAuth credentials, access tokens, refresh tokens, and other secrets are kept outside this public website and public repository.

Sharing

Atelier Digital does not resell Google Ads API data. Information may be processed by the authorized third-party services required to provide their APIs, subject to their own policies and terms.

Contact

No public professional contact address is currently published for Atelier Digital. Privacy enquiries may be sent using the professional contact method provided directly by the owner. A public contact address will be added here when one is made available.
